# Plan 2: Filters, Recurrence Controls, and Chore Editing

## Status and Goal

Planned; not implemented. Extend the [initial MVP](plan.md) so partners can find relevant chores, control recurring schedules, and update chore details. Keep the stack and development conventions in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Priority Order

1. Filter the board.
2. Pause, resume, or permanently stop recurring chores.
3. Edit chores and add assignment during creation.

## Board Filters

- Ownership options: **All**, **Mine**, and **Available**.
- **Mine** means only chores currently reserved for the signed-in partner; completed chores are excluded.
- Date options: **All dates**, **Overdue**, and **Due this week**.
- Combine one ownership option with one date option.
- Default to All and All dates, with a clear/reset action.
- Preserve the Available, Claimed, and Done board structure, with counts reflecting the filtered results.

Proposed details: weeks run Monday through Sunday in the household timezone. Due this week includes every due date in that calendar week; Overdue includes only unfinished chores due before today. Store filter selections in URL query parameters so refreshes and chore actions preserve the selected view.

## Recurring Chore Controls

- **Pause:** stop generating new occurrences until resumed.
- **Resume:** reactivate the original fixed schedule, skipping dates missed while paused.
- **Stop permanently:** end generation for that schedule with no resume action.
- Preserve existing occurrences, their owners, and completion history.
- Keep the existing claim, release, and completion actions available for existing unfinished occurrences.

Proposed details:

- Either partner can manage a schedule from a dedicated recurring-chores page, including schedules with no visible board cards.
- Show Active, Paused, or Stopped status and actions appropriate to each state. Confirm a permanent stop before applying it.
- On resume, the next eligible date is the first original scheduled date on or after the local resume date. Do not shift the original schedule anchor.
- Existing occurrences remain even if their dates fall during a pause or after a stop. This includes the future occurrence already generated by the MVP; explain this in the controls.
- Preserve eligible unfinished dates from before a pause. Track skipped intervals so later board visits never backfill dates missed during a pause, including across repeated pause/resume cycles.
- Continue generating occurrences on board visits or through the existing management command, without adding a background service.

## Editing and Assignment

### Edit chores

- Allow changing a chore's title, description, and required due date.

Proposed details: either partner can edit unfinished chores. Completed chores remain read-only. Editing a recurring occurrence affects only that occurrence; it does not change the series title, frequency, or date anchor. Preserve the occurrence's original schedule identity so changing its due date cannot cause generation to recreate it or collide with another occurrence. Editing does not change ownership or completion status.

### Assign during creation

- Add an **Assign to** field with **Unassigned** (default), **Me**, and **My partner**.
- Unassigned chores start as Available with no owner.
- Assigned chores start as Claimed by the selected household partner.
- The assigned partner can complete or release the chore using the existing workflow.

Unresolved choice — proposed default: for recurring chores, apply assignment only to the first occurrence (the initial due date). Later occurrences remain Available. This preserves the original pick-as-you-go behavior. The user has not yet selected whether assignment should instead apply to every occurrence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Mine excludes the other partner's chores, unassigned chores, and completed chores.
- Ownership and date filters combine correctly, including week boundaries and empty results.
- Filter selections remain active after claiming, releasing, or completing a chore.
- Paused and permanently stopped schedules generate no new occurrences.
- Resuming skips paused dates, preserves the fixed calendar, and creates no duplicates across repeated visits or pause/resume cycles.
- Existing occurrences and their ownership/history survive pause, resume, and stop actions.
- Stopped schedules cannot be resumed.
- Editing validates a nonempty title and required due date while preserving ownership and status.
- Editing a recurring occurrence does not shift future dates, recreate the original occurrence, or lose another occurrence.
- Creation supports unassigned and either-partner assignment with the correct initial status.
- Assignment accepts only the household's two partners.
- Existing MVP behavior and data continue to work after migrations.

## Outside This Plan

- Deleting chores or completed history.
- Editing an entire recurring series or changing its frequency.
- Reassigning existing chores through the edit form; release and claim remain available.
- Notifications, reminders, points, fairness reports, calendar integrations, or multiple households.
- A separate frontend, background scheduler, or database replacement.

## Implementation and Validation

Implement in priority order, retaining existing data through migrations. Add tests for combined filters, permissions, schedule transitions and skipped intervals, occurrence edits, and assignment. Run the Django tests and checks locally and in Docker, then verify the flows with both partner accounts. Update [README.md](../README.md) when the features are available.
